# On your phone

The phone app is named **ZigZag Planner**. The package name is `app.zigzag.planner`.

## Install from Android Studio

1. Plug in the phone and allow USB debugging when the phone asks.
2. In Android Studio, open the `android` folder of this project. If Studio is already open to that folder, use the device menu at the top.
3. Pick your phone, then Run.
4. The first launch is Today, with **Add something** ready.

You can also install the debug APK that Gradle writes to `android/app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk`.

## Share from Zoho Mail

In Zoho Mail, share the message as plain text and choose ZigZag Planner. The subject or first line becomes the title. The rest is the note. It lands in the inbox.

## On this device only

With no sign-in, tasks stay on that device. Settings says **Tasks stay on this device until you sign in.**

If you later sign in with Firebase, that account's tasks sync between the phone and the browser. Sign-in on the phone is email and password. Google sign-in in the installed app often does not open. Use email and password there.

## Light and dark

ZigZag follows the phone's light or dark setting. There is no theme picker.
