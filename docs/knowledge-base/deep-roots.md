# Deep Roots

ZigZag can show work that still needs you in Deep Roots Journal. It does not copy your journal writing. Titles, dates, and a link only.

## What shows up

New items land in the **inbox**, then go through triage. They do not jump onto Now.

| From Deep Roots | What you do in ZigZag |
|---|---|
| The current day of an active journal plan | **Open in Deep Roots**. There is no Done button. The card leaves when that day is finished in the journal. |
| Today's prayer, reading, study, or cohort check-off | **Done** checks it off in Deep Roots, once the connection is sending. |
| An unfinished action step | **Done** checks that step. |

ZigZag does not import the whole future rhythm, and it does not import a pile of earlier days.

A card from the journal says **From Deep Roots**.

## Connect

Settings → **Connect Deep Roots**.

Paste your Deep Roots user id and the site address, then **Save**. The status says **Connected** or **Not connected**. Today does not nag you to finish this.

Until the sync worker is running, you can paste a snapshot into **Import a Deep Roots snapshot** and tap **Save**. Those cards go to triage.

Check-offs you mark on this device wait until Deep Roots is connected, then they send. The note in Settings says that. Your tasks stay here either way.

## When a journal day is finished there

The matching ZigZag card moves to done wherever it was sitting. If it was Now, Now becomes empty. Next does not move up by itself.
