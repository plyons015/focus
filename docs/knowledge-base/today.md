# Today

Today is the only screen you need while you are working.

From top to bottom:

1. The time, and the next real thing if one is dated.
2. One **Now** card.
3. Up to two **Next** cards.
4. **Add something**.

The bar at the bottom moves you to This week, Someday, Projects, and Settings. Come back to Today when you are ready to work.

## Now

Now is the one thing you are on. It stays until you tap **Done**, **Park**, or **Swap**. A clock tick does not take it away.

- **Done** finishes it. If the card came from a Deep Roots journal day, there is no Done. Use **Open in Deep Roots** and finish the day there.
- **Park** sends it to This week. No reason required.
- **Swap** trades Now with the first Next item.

**Why this?** is a blank line for your own sentence. Leave it empty. ZigZag does not write it for you.

**Details** holds the context (Chaplain, Founder, or Personal), a due date, and a project.

If Now is empty, the card says **Nothing in Now.** Next stays where it is until you tap **Make this Now** or Swap.

## Next

Next is a short bench, not a list. Two cards at most.

**Make this Now** moves that card up. The old Now takes its place in Next.

If a third item tries to join Next, ZigZag asks: **Next already has two. Which one moves to This week?** You can also **Send the new one to This week**.

## End of the day

The next time you open ZigZag on a new day, anything still in Now or Next moves to This week. There is no banner and no count. Inbox, This week, and Someday stay where they are.

## Keyboard

On a computer, press `c` or Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac) to jump to **Add something**, unless you are already typing in a field.
